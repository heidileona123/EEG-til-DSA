Privacy policy

Anaesthesia and EEG – learning tool. Last updated: 25 October 2026.

In short

The app does not collect any personal data. It has no registration, login, tracking, analytics or advertising.

What is stored

The app remembers which sub-tab you last opened in each tab, so that you return there next time. This is stored only locally on your device and is not sent anywhere. It is deleted when you delete the app or your browser data.

Internet

All content, including the fonts, is contained in the app, so it does not fetch anything from the internet. Links to source articles and guidelines open external websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Contact

If you send feedback by e-mail, your address is used only to reply to you.
